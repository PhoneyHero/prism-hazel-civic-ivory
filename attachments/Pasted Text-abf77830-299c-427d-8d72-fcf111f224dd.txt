**0. DELETE THE CURRENT MOVER**

Remove every wander-roulette, random-direction-at-intersection, “pick a neighbor and hope,” and any stuck-in-dead-end dither. Replace with the systems below. If a unit has no legal goal it idles 0.4s then picks a new goal. It never oscillates two tiles.

Grid is 4-direction only. No diagonals. No walking through solid walls. Combat and shots are cardinal with a clear lane.

**1. SHARED PATH TOOL**

Write one function: `route(from, to) -> list of dug tiles`.

- Only dug / air tiles are walkable.
- Prefer the shortest walk.
- If the target is unreachable, return empty and the unit picks another goal.
- Recalc when the grid changes (a wall dug, a wall smashed by a hero) or every 1.5s while moving.
- Units do not stand still more than 0.6s unless their role says rest / bud / cocoon / sleep / web.

**2. HERO BRAIN (always exploring)**

Heroes spawn at the stair. Goal order, top wins:

1. If they have line-of-corridor on the Warden → route to Warden. On contact they pick him up and route to the stair at 60% speed (Thief 2s at full). Camera snap `WARDEN SEIZED` once.
2. Else if a monster is in 3-tile cardinal range → route to that monster and fight.
3. Else if an unexplored dug tile exists → route to the nearest **unvisited** dug tile. Mark tiles visited as they walk. Do not re-visit a dead-end corridor unless a new opening was dug on it.
4. Party intel: if two heroes stand on adjacent tiles they copy visited + dead-end flags. No long-range radio.
5. If no unvisited tiles remain and Warden not seen → route toward the farthest dug tile from the stair (deep search).
6. **Stuck ≥ 2.5s** in the same cell or same two-cell wobble: frustration += 1. At 3, smash one orthogonal solid wall (0 player Dig Power). That tile becomes air. If it held N/M, split that value into remaining solid neighbors. Then reset frustration and re-route.
7. Stamina still burns (walk 1 / melee 2 / skill 8). At 0 they die and leave Remains.
8. Skills fire on cooldown when a monster is in lane. Skill splash still writes N or M into orthogonal walls.

Heroes never stand in a dead end “thinking.” They turn around on the next tick and take the next unvisited branch.

**3. MONSTER GOALS (each family has a job)**

Every monster always has exactly one goal. Re-pick when the goal is done or invalid.

**Loamite (nutrient truck)**  
Goal: richest orthogonal wall in 4 tiles that still has N, else poorest wall to dump. Walk the route, suck ~5, walk, spit ~5. Vitality ticks. Full belly or vitality 0 → root in place as Bud (radius-1 wall drain) → Bloom (radius-2, bites adjacent heroes) → natural death hatches 1–4 empty Loamites OR Smash slams stored N into walls (no hatch).

**Wisp (mana truck)**  
Same as Loamite but M only. Does not root into a flower. Dies → 50% M to walls.

**Gnawling (hunter → cocoon → fly)**  
Goal while crawling: nearest Loamite or Bud/Bloom in 3 tiles, else wander to a random dug tile at the edge of a cluster (not coin-flip every step — pick a destination 6–10 tiles away and route there). After enough eats: freeze as Cocoon. Hatch timer → Fly. Fly goal: nearest Loamite, or a quiet corner to drop one egg, then hunt again. Scalekin can eat crawler, cocoon, or fly.

**Scalekin (feed + nest)**  
Hungry or HP missing → route to nearest Gnawling / cocoon / fly and eat. Full HP and Loamite count ≥ 4 may still eat Gnawlings. Full HP and Loamite count < 4 → do not eat Loamites. Nest goal: find a dug rectangle at least 2×3; stand there; lay an egg on a timer after meals. Sleep only after a fight or a full meal, max 8s, never on spawn. Wake if hit or prey adjacent.

**Hexwife line**  
Goal: nearest Wisp or Gnawling to eat, else patrol a long straight dug hall. Every few tiles if the floor is bare, leave a web token that slows heroes 40%. Ranged attack cardinal only, clear lane.

**Wyrm**  
Goal: hold a straight horizontal dug run at least 4 tiles long. Walk that run only. Breath is horizontal. Attack if a hero is on that run. Do not wander the whole map. If no 4-tile run exists, sit and act as a turret. Eats almost anything when hungry. Death / age → egg. Hatch tier follows mana eaten.

**Horned One**  
Goal: the Warden’s room. Stand there. Aura +DEF to allies in that room. Weak melee if a hero enters.

**Rattle**  
Goal: patrol like an explorer — pick the nearest unvisited dug tile in a 8-tile memory unique to that Rattle, then the next. Fight heroes in range. No N/M belly. Death → Remains, 0 splash. Mana lifespan 45s / 90s by tier. Wisps touching Remains can redden the pile (5 merges → Red Rattle). Max 4 living Rattles.
