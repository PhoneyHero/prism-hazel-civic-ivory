**4. WARDEN RULES**

- No heroes until Warden is seated. Auto-seat at farthest real corridor if player never taps Throne.
- Move allowed only in prep / ECO WAIT, **once per window**.

- Grab = drag toward stair at 60% speed. Kill carrier → drop on that tile. Loss only if carrier steps on the entrance stair. Dead chain + living hero inside = loss.

**5. REMAINS AND RATTLES**

- Remains **never despawn**. No 20s, no 30s.
- Tap Remains with Pick: spend 0 Dig Power. **Do not splash nutrients.** Spawn one Rattle.
- Rattle has no stomach. It **siphons the nearest walls** the way a block is read. Whatever it drank when it rose sets its breed:
  - mostly N → bone soldier
  - mostly M → pale caster bone
  - mixed → plated bone
- Rattle mana/life ticks down. At 0 it collapses back into Remains. Player may raise it again. Each raise drinks the walls again (can starve Hexwife soil if you spam raises).
- Rattle does not eat the food chain.

**6. DEATH TAX AND LOAMITE EXCEPTION**

- Ecosystem unit dies (age, starve, hero, cull): **50%** of stored N or M goes to nearest walls. Other 50% gone. That is the leak.
- Eating does not transfer 100%. Part of the meal only resets the eater’s drain.
- Loamite while alive moves **100%** of what it carried from one wall-adjacent cell to another wall. That is the only lossless pump.
- Loamite that dies of age/starve becomes **Wiltbloom** on the floor. Wiltbloom is a pickup: Pick-smash or a high-tier step converts it to a full wall dump (no 50% tax). Heroes stepping on it destroy it for nothing.
- Closed map with no new walls dug and no hero corpses → total collapse. That is required.

**7. FAMILIES, IDEAL ROOMS, AI**

Loamite: walks cardinal, turns at walls. Hunts high-N walls, dumps on low-N walls. Ideal = long H / O / T corridors. Short life. Divide when full.

Gnawling: ignores soil. Hunts Loamite. Pupates in a dead-end after N eats. Ideal = loops with moss traffic. Swarm.

Scalekin: hunts Gnawling. Lays eggs only in dug **2×3** rooms. Ideal = rooms off the hero path. Slow. Starving Scalekin cannibalize Gnawling first, then each other.

Hexwife line: ranged **cardinal only**, clear lane, no through-walls. Shots leave **web on floor** that slows heroes 40%. Eats Wisp and Gnawling. Ideal = long straight halls aimed at the stair.

Wyrm: huge HP. Breath is **horizontal only** (left/right). Eats almost anything. Ideal = one-tile choke. Starvation → egg. Next Wyrm from that egg can mutate.

Horned One: weak hit. Aura in the same room: +DEF to allies. Ideal = the Warden room.

All units: HP drains on a timer. Eating is the only refill. Empty stomach = fast flash-drain + AI flips to cannibal / desperation. Old age still kills a fed unit eventually.

Combat: orthogonal only. No diagonal. No through dirt.

**8. MUTATIONS (next birth only)**
