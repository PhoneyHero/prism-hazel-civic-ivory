Update the existing Loam Lord game. Keep the current art, names, and feel. Fix the bugs and deepen the simulation to match What Did I Do to Deserve This, My Lord!? 2 systems. Do not add a backend. Do not add accounts.
1. OVERLORD MUST EXIST BEFORE RAID
The raid timer must not start, and heroes must not spawn, until an Overlord is on a dug tile.
If the player never taps Throne, when the prep timer hits zero the game AUTO-PLACES the Overlord on the best dug tile: farthest walk distance from the entrance stair, not a 1-tile stub, preferably at the end of a corridor.
Show a short banner: “Throne claimed.”
Player may still move the throne twice after auto-place.
Heroes path to the Overlord. They never wander forever in an empty map.
2. CAPTURE IS A DRAG, NOT INSTANT LOSS
When a hero reaches the Overlord they do not win instantly.
That hero picks up the Overlord and walks back toward the entrance stair at reduced speed.
Other heroes escort or keep fighting.
If dungeon monsters reduce that carrier to 0 HP before they reach the stair, the Overlord drops on that tile. Player can tap Throne to reseat him (uses one of the remaining moves if any).
Loss only if the carrier steps onto the entrance stair while holding the Overlord.
This is the only lose-to-capture condition. Chain-wipe while a living hero is still in the hollow is still a loss.
3. DIG POWER
Raise starting Dig Power by +10 on every stage (Chamber keeps its large budget).
Unused Dig Power does not need to carry between stages unless already implemented.
4. SOIL, WALLS, AND NUTRIENT / MANA RULES
Every undug block is a soil cell with two hidden integers: nutrient 0–3+ and mana 0–3+. Values are predetermined per stage layout, not random each tap.
Birth table when a block is dug:
NUTRIENT CHAIN (5 births)
N1 → Moss (walks cardinal, turns at walls, pulls nutrient from current cell and dumps onto the next cell and onto adjacent UNDUG wall faces)
N2 → Crawler (hunts Moss, pupates after enough eats)
N3 → Lizard (hunts Crawlers, lays eggs only in a dug 2×3 room)
N2+N3 mix or high N after Lizard presence → Drake (apex, eats Crawler, Lizard, Witch)
Bud/Flower is the Moss upgrade when a Moss dies fat on high-N soil (counts as the fifth nutrient form)
MANA CHAIN (5 births, match nutrient count)
M1 → Spirit (same job as Moss but for mana)
M2 → Witch (ranged cardinal shots, eats Spirit and Crawler)
M3 → Vampire (stronger Witch)
M3+ → Succubus (faster breed, fragile)
High M after Witch line exists → Nimbus (fifth mana form)
Drake remains the shared top predator.
Walls matter. Nutrients and mana live in undug neighboring blocks, not only on walked floors.
When you smash a monster, a fungus, or a hero corpse: splash N or M onto the 4 adjacent tiles, INCLUDING undug walls. Those walls level up. Digging them later births the matching tier.
When a Moss/Spirit walks along a corridor, they also leach from and paint the wall tiles they brush.
Hero death: Warrior / brute drops NUTRIENT on adjacent floors and walls. Mage / priest / witch-hero drops MANA. Mixed class splits both.
Monster death: nutrient-born splash N, mana-born splash M.
Heat map must show wall stores, not just open floor.
5. HEROES
More hero types. Minimum roster: Swordsman, Archer, Mage, Priest, Knight, Thief.
Warrior-types drop nutrient. Caster-types drop mana. Knight drops both small.
Stages use multiple waves. Wave 2 and 3 spawn only after wave 1 is dead or after a mid-stage timer, whichever the stage script says.
Later stages use higher HP/ATK heroes and overlapping waves.
Heroes path only on dug tiles. They fight the first monster they touch. They prioritize the Overlord once they have line of corridor to him.
6. STAGES
Replace the tiny set with at least these:
First Hollow — 1 wave, 1 weak swordsman. Tutorial soil. Auto-place still applies.
Mossworks — 2 waves. Teach nutrient splash on walls.
Twin Gate — 2 waves, swordsman + mage so both resources drop.
Witchwell — mana-heavy walls. 2 waves, mage first.
Bone Procession — 3 waves, Knight on wave 3.
Deep Throne — 3 waves, mixed roster, stronger ATK. Tight Dig Power even with +10.
The Chamber — sandbox, huge Dig Power, player taps “Summon raid” when ready. No lose-for-empty-throne until they summon.
Each story stage: prep time → mandatory Overlord on grid → wave 1 → wave 2/3 → win if all heroes dead and Overlord not carried out.
7. MUTATION (keep, make it visible)
Stress clocks per species:
Starvation deaths → next birth of that species is Bloated (high HP, slow).
Eaten-by-predator deaths → Twisted (poison or sleep on hit).
Killed-by-hero deaths → Dire (flat stat bump).
Banner “ominous” when a clock trips. Almanac logs the variant.
8. UI
Dig Power, raid timer, wave counter, Horde Power, Overlord state: SEATED / CARRIED / DROPPED.
If carried, show a drag progress pip toward the stair.
Heat map toggle for N and M including walls.
Do not let the player start a raid with 0 Overlord on the board.
9. ACCEPTANCE
A player who never taps Throne still gets an auto-placed Overlord and cannot win by idling an empty maze.
A player who loses the Overlord mid-fight can still kill the carrier and recover him.
Breaking a wall after a hero corpse nearby can birth Spirit or Witch, not only Moss.
Five nutrient forms and five mana forms exist and can appear in Chamber.
Dig Power is +10 versus current build on story stages.
