**A. EMPTY HOLLOW IS NOT A LOSS**

Census at 0 living monsters is not defeat. Defeat only if a hero carrying the Warden steps on the entrance stair. A carrier can still die mid-drag. Then the Warden drops and the wave continues.

**B. WARDEN PETITION**

After a wave is fully dead, wait **3 seconds**. Then:

- Wave 1 only: snap camera to Warden, then an old-school RPG bar (portrait + text + two choices), same layout as “Aye / Nay”:

`WARDEN: The hollow held. Shall I bless the next brood with what the pick still has?`  
`Aye` / `Nay`

Aye opens upgrades. Nay closes.

- First Aye also says: `Only what is born after you pay is blessed.`

**C. NAVIGATION**

Remove left-hand wall-follow. Restore the previous mover (A* / last pre-left-hand version). Heroes share map intel **only when they stand on adjacent tiles** (1-block range): dead-end flags and seen tiles. No radio across the maze.

**D. LOAMITE LIFECYCLE**

Loamite is a state machine, not a simple death.

1. `WALK` — vitality ticks down. Sucks ~5 N from rich walls, dumps into poor walls.
2. Vitality ≤ 0 **or** belly full → root on current air tile → `BUD`. Cannot move. Sucks orthogonal walls, radius 1.
3. If it drinks enough → `BLOOM` (Wiltbloom / blossom). Radius 2. Bites a hero on an adjacent tile.
4. Bloom lifespan ends naturally → hatch `floor(storedN / 10)` new Loamites (min 1, max 4) with full HP and empty bellies. No corpse.
5. Gnawling that eats a Bud/Bloom takes the stored N and may pupate.
6. Player Smash on Bud/Bloom: no hatch. Slam stored N into orthogonal walls.

This is the low-tier faucet. Path bloom chance on empty dug tiles is **0.5% every 12s**, cap 6 extra flowers. Smash those the same way.

**E. PREDATORS AND SLEEP**

- If a predator is below full HP it **always** eats valid prey to heal. No skip.
- If it is full HP and Loamite count < 4, it may skip Loamites and wander / sleep.
- Do **not** put newborns to sleep on spawn. Sleep only after a fight or after a full meal, and only Scalekin / Wyrm. Sleep has a cap (8s). Hit or adjacent prey wakes them. Fix the “born asleep for a long time” bug.

**F. RATTLES (Skeleman rules)**

Remains are not soil. They never bleed N/M on death or on raise.

On hero death store `manaAtDeath` and class on the pile.

Wisp that touches Remains is consumed. `spiritCount++`. At 5 the pile turns **red**.

Tap Remains with Pick (0 DP), no splash:

- red pile → Red Rattle (fast, high ATK)
- manaAtDeath high or Mage/Priest → caster Rattle
- mid → plated Rattle
- low / Swordsman / Thief → bone Rattle

Living Rattle burns internal mana (~45s tier1, ~90s plated/death-knight). Nearby Wisps drain it faster. At 0 it collapses to Remains again. Re-raise allowed.

Max **4** living Rattles. Further taps do nothing until one collapses.

Rattle death: Remains only. **0** N/M to walls.
